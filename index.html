Through decades of honest craftsmanship and deep respect for Telugu rituals, he built AD LIVE EVENTS into Tirupati’s most respected and cherished photography house. His distinctive eye for detail, emotional authenticity, and cultural reverence transformed simple event coverage into timeless works of art.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking Tirupati's most trusted photography studio.

How early should we book our Telugu wedding date? +

For peak Telugu wedding seasons and auspicious Muhurtham dates (especially Magha, Phalguna, and Vaishakha masams), we strongly recommend reserving your date 3 to 6 months in advance. Because founder Anil Kumar personally oversees team allocations, our peak dates fill up very quickly.

Do you cover events outside Tirupati (Chittoor, Nellore, Kadapa, Chennai, Bangalore)? +

Yes! While our flagship studio is beside B-New Mobiles on Tilak Road, Tirupati, our crew regularly travels across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu for destination weddings and traditional celebrations.

What is your typical turnaround time for wedding films and albums? +

We deliver an initial 1-minute Instagram Teaser within 48 to 72 hours so you can share the joy immediately. The complete master color-graded 4K cinematic film and high-res digital gallery are delivered within 14 days. Premium heirloom leather albums are handcrafted and delivered within 3 to 4 weeks upon client layout approval.

Do you provide 1080p60 Live Streaming for relatives overseas (USA, UK, Australia)? +

Yes. We use professional Blackmagic ATEM live switchers and multi-camera setups with dedicated high-speed cellular bonding to stream private or public feeds in crisp 1080p60 on YouTube / Facebook with pristine lossless audio so your relatives abroad feel like they are sitting in the mandapam.

Can we customize our photography and cinematography package? +

Absolutely! Every family's rituals and timelines are unique. You can mix and match drone aerials, candid stills, traditional photo albums, and extra cinematographers. Click "✦ Check Date" or WhatsApp us directly to craft a bespoke quote tailored to your budget.
